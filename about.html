Jun (Vincent) He · Millbrook School · Class of 2027

I’m Vincent He, a student at Millbrook School interested in astrophysics, cosmology, and how observations help us understand processes we cannot see directly. My research spans supermassive black-hole mergers, solar coronal heating, and sulfur K-edge XANES. Across these projects, I have reviewed scientific literature, compared evidence, and used reference spectra to examine an unknown sample.

Outside research, I make science media, diagrams, and music. I founded an astrophysics outreach initiative and a peer research group, where I help other students develop questions, interpret evidence, and present their work. With Millbrook’s Physics & Engineering Club, I have also worked on designing, testing, and improving a model rocket.

Sport is another part of my week: cross country, track, and squash. My 5K personal best has improved from 24:30 to 19:36. On campus, I lead a card game club, welcome prospective families, and support incoming international students. I also volunteer at Trevor Zoo.
