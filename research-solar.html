Four signatures to look for

Intensity variability

Emission varies as plasma heats and cools. Is the heating impulsive rather than steady?

Temperature-dependent time lags

Emission peaks progress through cooler temperature channels. Is a heating–cooling sequence visible?

Emission-measure distribution

The amount of plasma at each temperature reflects heating and cooling. Does the thermal structure match model predictions?

Very hot plasma

Impulsive energy release can briefly produce plasma above 5 MK. Are signatures of sufficiently energetic heating present?

The takeaway

Heating and cooling patterns in the reviewed studies are consistent with the nanoflare model.

The evidence is indirect and partly overlapping; it does not establish nanoflares as the sole primary heating mechanism.
