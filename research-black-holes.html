01 / Context

Why this question?

Black-hole mergers connect the evolution of binaries with the evolution of galaxies. This review considers how different kinds of observations could help connect those scales and inform cosmology.

02 / Contribution

What I did.

I authored a six-page review of supermassive black-hole binary evolution, the final-parsec problem, and links among black-hole mergers, galaxy formation, and cosmology.

Read the methods
  • Synthesized seven scholarly sources on binary evolution and observational approaches.
  • Compared low-frequency gravitational-wave detection with pulsar timing arrays and LISA.
  • Examined multimessenger and standard-siren approaches to constraining black-hole properties, the Hubble constant, and dark energy.

03 / Interpretation

What the work tells us.

The review brings together theoretical and observational approaches to black-hole mergers. It considers how gravitational-wave and electromagnetic observations can address complementary questions about binaries and cosmology.

What I cannot claim.

This is a literature review. It does not report a new gravitational-wave detection, original observational data, or an independent measurement of the Hubble constant or dark energy.
